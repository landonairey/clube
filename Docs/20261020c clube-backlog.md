## The chisel

- [ ] **Carve one voxel at a time** — when the chisel is used, any mesh in the voxel is removed and replaced by air, so where there was neighbouring material there are sharp vertical walls going down to the newly chiselled voxel. The chisel removes material on the voxel grid, one voxel at a time, and only removes mesh within the highlighted voxel. The chisel never adds mesh material. → **GL92**
- [ ] **Chisel icon** — the chisel icon isn't very recognizable; change it. → **GL93**
- [ ] **Voxel highlight** — instead of the tool's impact area shaded on the ground, the chisel highlights the voxel you're looking at. Not too bright: you still need to see through it. → **GL94**
