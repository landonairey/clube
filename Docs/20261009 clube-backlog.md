## Parked for later (with skills)

- [ ] **Prospecting reveals ore** — the prospecting skill unlocks seeing ores. At lower levels, ore in rock just looks like rock; once mined, its contents show a ? until the player can tell. → **PS6** (6B)

## Ore contents

- [ ] **Mixed ore** — mined ore has mixed contents: copper ore mined is 80% rock and 20% ore by volume. The split changes with the grade of the node cluster it spawned in, and can have more than two contents. Later skills will help separate ore from rock. → **GL32** (first pass of PK1); separation → **PR7**

## Yield

- [ ] **Smelting yield and scale loss** — smelting mixed ore directly lowers the yield: 1 volume of ore with 20% ore content might only yield 50% because of its grade, so the copper bun is 0.2 × 0.5 = 10% of the starting volume. Hammering the bun into an ingot loses more to scale, starting at 20%; the blacksmithing skill improves the ingot yield. All up: 1 volume of 80/20 rock to copper ore → 0.2 copper ore → 0.1 bun at 50% yield → 0.08 ingot after 20% scale loss. → **GL33**

## Clay

- [ ] **Clay deposits and bricks** — clay deposits spawn at the surface, flush with the terrain and reaching down a little. Clay piles in squares like rock. Mined clay left out on the ground for 2 days dries into brick; it can also be fired into brick in the furnace. → **GL34**

## Fuel

- [ ] **Furnace fuel** — the furnace takes fuel to burn. Biomass such as wood and leaves can be used, but leaves burn much quicker than wood. A stack of fuel goes in the fuel slot, and the furnace keeps pulling a new one into its burn-down bar as long as there are items to process. → **GL35** (first pass of HF1)
