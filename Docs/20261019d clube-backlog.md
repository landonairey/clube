# clube — Backlog (2026-10-19, fourth batch)

Notes from late on 2026-10-19, kept as they were given (spelling tidied, grouped under headings). They are organized in `Docs/clube-objectives.md` as Milestone 2's thirteenth pass; the IDs below say where each one went. Like the earlier backlogs, this file is a snapshot: change the objectives doc, not this.

## The chisel

- [ ] **Bronze chisel** — a new tool you can make. It cuts any material into sharp, square-edged voxels. → **GL87** (first pass of TL2's chisel)
- [ ] **Mixed meshing** — after cutting, the terrain in that chunk becomes a mix of square voxels and marching voxels (see the objectives list for more: MX2). → **GL86** (first pass of MX2)
- [ ] **Flat Rock** — ultimately the chisel is for cutting one of the large surface rocks into a flat working surface, the Flat Rock, a precursor to the anvil. → **GL88** (first pass of WS1's large stone)

## Debugging

- [ ] **Debug material chunk** — a debug ability that makes a flat surface at the player's ground level and fills it with piles of each type of material item. → **GL89**
