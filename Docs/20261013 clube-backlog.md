## The beginning path: skills

- [ ] **Main skills** — the main beginning path is upgrading these skills: Crafting, Woodcutting, Mining, Smelting. → **GL47**
- [ ] Punch a tree with your hand to get wood (woodcutting xp). → **GL47**
- [ ] Use the tree stump to craft a pointy stick (crafting xp). → **GL49**
- [ ] The pointy stick harvests better than your hand (woodcutting or mining xp). → **GL49**
- [ ] Collect enough stone to build a furnace (mining xp and crafting xp). → **GL53**
- [ ] Collect cassiterite (tin ore) deposits, some on the surface (mining xp). → **GL51**
- [ ] Smelt cassiterite ore into a tin bun (smelting xp). → **GL51**

## Unlocks

- [ ] **Stone pick** — with enough xp you can unlock crafting a stone pick, a stone on a stick, which gathers hard material like stone and ore better. → **GL50**, **GL48**
- [ ] **Seeing copper ore** — with enough experience you unlock seeing chalcopyrite and malachite (copper ore). The coloured ore veins aren't visible in the terrain mesh until then; mined before that, they give a "?" ore. → **GL54** (first pass of **PS6**)
- [ ] **Bronze** — with enough smelting xp you unlock the first alloy recipe: tin and copper buns come together to make a bronze bun; tin and copper ingots make a bronze ingot; a bronze bun can be hammered into a bronze ingot. → **GL55**
- [ ] **Campfire first** — actually you make a campfire first, which can be used to smelt down that tin ore. The player needs to unlock crafting a furnace, which can then smelt tin too. → **GL52**, **GL53**
