# clube — Backlog (2026-10-07)

Ideas and changes asked for on 2026-10-07, after the first loop playtest, kept as they were noted. They are organized in `Docs/clube-objectives.md` as Milestone 2's second pass; the IDs below say where each one went. Like the earlier backlogs, this file is a snapshot: change the objectives doc, not this.

---

## Dropping

- [ ] **Piles by material** — dropping soft to medium material piles up in conical piles; dropping hard material drops in a square cube pile within a build square. → **GL17**

## Tools

- [ ] **Tool cursor, perimeter only** — the crosshairs only on the edges on the perimeter of the tool's area of impact: 4 for the hand, 4 for the pickaxe; small right-angle crosshairs instead of crosses. → **GL18**

## Stations and screens

- [ ] **Furnace takes a stack** — the furnace accepts a stack of items as input, processes them one after another and stacks the results in the output. → **GL19**
- [ ] **Slot screens** — the anvil, furnace and merchant screens use item slots like the hotbar. → **GL20**

## Terrain

- [ ] **Stone on steep slopes** — steep mountain slopes above a certain elevation are stone instead of grass and dirt. → **GL21**
- [ ] **Surface rocks** — baby steps for collecting: sprinkle single, double or triple samples of a rock material on the grass surface, drawing small mesh bumps that look like rocks, scattered randomly. → **GL22**

## Building

- [ ] **Placeable stations** — start with the furnace, anvil and merchant table in the inventory and place them on the terrain, snapping to the build grid. → **GL23**
- [ ] **Build mode grid** — while placing, show the build grid on the terrain, only a local part of it: the centre cell lit, the grid fading out radially in a circle; hide it again once placed. → **GL23**
- [ ] **B to place, hold B to pick up** — press B to place the item; press and hold B while looking at a placed one to pick it back up. → **GL23**
