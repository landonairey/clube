## Furnaces

- [ ] **Furnace state of health** — the furnace degrades in health over time. It can be repaired with its base building material back to some state of health. The state of health fades from 100% based on cycle usage. → **HF6**
- [ ] **Furnace build material** — a furnace built with loose stones breaks more easily than one built with clay. Stones repair the stone furnace, clay repairs the clay furnace. A clay furnace gets hotter than a stone furnace. → **HF2**, **HF7**
- [ ] **Brick furnace** — sand + clay turned into bricks makes an even more durable furnace that can get even hotter. → **HF7**
- [ ] **Silica brick furnace** — a slight boost after further refining sand into silica to make silica bricks. → **HF7**
- [ ] **Ceramic furnace** — another step up is ceramic made with alumina + silica, which can handle high enough heat to enable steel making. → **HF7**
- [ ] **Refractory supply chain** — bauxite ore nodes in the map, refined to alumina, which can then take further additives to enhance certain application performance properties. → **HF8**, **X1**

## Trade

- [ ] **Player-owned shop** — set up to sell items to locals or travellers. An appearance bonus from fine linens, carpets and high-quality goods on display nudges the average selling price higher. → **TR7**

## Materials and tools ladder

- [ ] **Early ores** — introduce some early-game, low-skill-level ores. Tin is a good one. → **ML4**, **X1**
- [ ] Wood makes a basic stick and spear for basic tools. → **ML1**
- [ ] Combining stone with the previous wooden implements gets to a basic stone hatchet and hammer. → **ML1**
- [ ] A minor boost from using leather strips for strapping increases durability. → **ML1**
- [ ] Copper is the next step, for copper tools. Super low yield with cold forging; hot forging increases the yield. Hot forging requires at least a stone furnace. → **ML2**
- [ ] Clay can be used in the stone furnace to make basic pottery. → **ML3**
- [ ] Tin unlocks alloying with copper for bronze tools, and tin alone makes basic jewellery and decorative plates. → **ML4**
- [ ] Coal for smelting fuel. → **ML5**, **HF1**
- [ ] Iron requires at least a clay furnace, and coal for fuel. → **ML6**
- [ ] Zinc unlocks brass by alloying with copper, for technical implements, measurement pieces, clockwork and instruments, and eventually galvanized steel. → **ML7**
- [ ] Lead unlocks weights for scales. → **ML8**
- [ ] Silver for jewellery and plateware. → **ML9**
- [ ] Steel requires at least a ceramic furnace, and coal and bellows for fuel. → **ML10**
- [ ] Cast iron from iron, silica and coal (carbon): a lower melting point and complex moulds. → **ML11**
- [ ] Gold for jewellery, and it unlocks travelling merchants between cities. → **ML12**
- [ ] Nickel for advanced alloys, stainless steel. → **ML13**
- [ ] Cobalt for stronger equipment. → **ML13**
- [ ] Platinum for high-value crafting, and it unlocks stock market manipulation. → **ML13**
- [ ] Aluminium from bauxite. → **ML13**
- [ ] Titanium for lightweight equipment. → **ML13**
- [ ] Tungsten for heavy-duty tools and alloys, tool steel. → **ML13**
- [ ] Iridium is ultra rare. → **ML13**
- [ ] Bone as a sharp material in the beginning: sharper than stone but less durable. Makes a basic bone sewing needle. → **ML1**
- [ ] Glass after unlocking refining silica from sand. → **ML14**, **DV1**
- [ ] Limestone unlocks concrete with sand, crushed stone and water. → **ML14**
- [ ] Soft rubber from rubber trees. → **ML14**
- [ ] Sulfur unlocks vulcanized rubber. → **ML14**
- [ ] Gunpowder from just sulfur and coal, or should we go the potassium nitrate route? It could be more satisfying. → **ML14**, Open questions
- [ ] Mercury is somewhere in here, for advanced scientific instruments. → **ML14**
- [ ] Tiers of wood from soft wood to hard wood. Improves the value of the crafted item. → **ML15**
- [ ] Tiers of stone unlock different masonry items and infrastructure. → **ML16**
- [ ] Tiers of gemstones. Garnet and diamond for tool hardness; all others for jewellery. Pearls from clams. Track ivory, jet, amber, pearls and mother of pearl; for simplicity, mother of pearl is a rarer drop from clams than pearls. → **ML17**, **X3**

## Workstations

- [ ] **Workstation ladder** — the tree stump is the first crafting worktop. A large stone next, for sharpening tools, breaking rock and hammering on. After that, a wood plank workbench. → **WS1**
- [ ] **Tools stored at the worktop** — tools crafted are stored at the crafting worktop. The better the quality of the tools, the better the quality of the crafted items and equipment. A grind wheel crafted with high-quality tools produces even better tool sharpening. → **WS2**
- [ ] **Workplace bonus** — a hidden bonus if the workplace is on a flat surface, and additional bonuses for harder platform or floor materials. → **WS3**
- [ ] **Stations built into the terrain** — equipment and work areas are built with the materials right into the terrain mesh, e.g. stone furnaces are cobblestone material on the ground. They are protected from being destroyed by accident while digging, but you can click to unlock them and dig them up for their raw materials. Unlocking one removes the ability to press E to use the workplace. → **WS4**, **WS5**
