## Unknown ores

- [ ] **One "unknown" slot per ore** — ores collected before they're discovered all piled up under one common "unknown" item, and after discovering them with a higher mining level they didn't separate into their different ores. Each undiscovered ore should enter the inventory under its own "unknown" slot: mining silver, gold, chalcopyrite and malachite gives four different unknown slots. → **GL90**
